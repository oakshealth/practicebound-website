Why Does Preceptorship Cost Money?

Many students ask: "Why do I have to pay for preceptorship?" Here's why education from experienced professionals deserves fair compensation.

What You're Actually Paying For

1. Expert Time & Experience

Every preceptor charging for their services is investing their time—time that could be spent seeing more patients or running their practice. My 10+ years of experience didn't come free. That expertise has value.

2. One-on-One Mentoring

In nursing school, one professor teaches 30-100 students. In preceptorship, I dedicate focused attention to YOU as an individual learner. That personalized education model costs money.

3. Clinical Liability & Responsibility

When you see patients under my supervision, I'm responsible for every decision and outcome. I carry professional liability for your actions. That responsibility deserves compensation.

4. Continuous Feedback & Coaching

I spend time weekly giving you feedback, identifying gaps, and coaching you toward competency. This isn't passive observation—it's active teaching.

5. Practice Management Expertise

You're not just learning clinical skills. You're learning how to run a practice, manage patients, handle billing, and understand healthcare systems. That's real-world business knowledge.

6. Board Exam Preparation

My goal isn't just to get you through clinical hours—it's to prepare you to PASS your boards on the first attempt. That targeted preparation takes intentional planning.

The Academic Parallel

I teach nursing students at a highly regarded private university in Durham, NC. That university PAYS me to precept and teach because they understand that education has value. Why should NP/DNP preceptorship be different? The work is identical. The impact is arguably greater—NP students go on to be healthcare providers who make diagnoses, prescribe medications, and lead practices.

The Bottom Line

Quality education costs money. It always has. What we're doing is making quality, ethical preceptorship affordable—50-75% cheaper than other platforms—while maintaining the highest standards.

Your preceptor's time, expertise, and accountability are valuable. Paying for them is an investment in YOUR future as a healthcare provider. 💙

Frequently Asked Questions

What is clinical preceptorship?
Clinical preceptorship is one-on-one mentoring from an experienced NP/DNP provider. You see patients under their supervision, receive feedback, and learn real-world clinical practice. It's different from shadowing or mentorship—you actively participate in patient care.
How long does preceptorship take?
Duration depends on your program requirements: FNP students typically need 8-16 weeks, DNP students 12-24 weeks, and RN-BSN students 40 hours. We customize to YOUR timeline and YOUR hours per week.
Can I do part-time preceptorship?
Absolutely! We offer flexible scheduling. Whether you can do 8 hours per week or 20 hours per week, we adapt to your life. Many students balance other work or coursework during preceptorship.
What's included in the price?
Your price includes: real clinical supervision, weekly feedback sessions, mid and final evaluations, board exam preparation, and access to your preceptor. There are no hidden fees.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes! We offer flexible payment options. For example, FNP program: $800 down + $320/month for 5 months. We want to make this accessible.
Will I see real patients?
Yes! You'll see actual patients in our established primary care practice. You'll develop real clinical skills with real patient continuity, not simulations or theory.
What's the board exam bonus?
If you pass your NP/DNP boards on the first attempt, you get a $200 bonus from us. We're that confident in our supervision model and your success.
Are you expanding to other states?
Yes! We're currently in North Carolina and expanding to Virginia (Q1 2026) and Vermont (Q2 2026). Contact us to get on the waitlist for your state.
